Applying for e-billing permission on the IRD Taxpayer Portal, step by step
  1. Log in to the IRD Taxpayer Portal with the café's PAN login.
  2. Open Electronic Billing → Electronic Billing User Permission and start a new application.
  3. Enter the software details exactly as below:
    Software name{{.Software}}
    Version{{.Version}}
    Producer{{.Producer.Company}} (PAN {{.Producer.PAN}})
    Enlisted No.{{or .Producer.EnlistedNo "(ask the producer)"}}
    ServerCloud — {{.Datacenter.Company}}, {{.Datacenter.Location}} ({{.Datacenter.Domain}})
    Billing outlets{{range $i, $b := .Branches}}{{if $i}}, {{end}}{{$b.Name}}{{if $b.OutletCode}} ({{$b.OutletCode}}){{end}}{{end}}
  4. Print the submitted form, sign and stamp it.
  5. Take to the tax office ({{or .Taxpayer.TaxOffice "your Inland Revenue Office"}}): the printed form, the Annex 3 application, the Annex 2 consent letter, the Annex 7 agreement, the server-location notice, copies of PAN, registration and the previous year's tax clearance, the producer's sales bill to the café, and the sample prints (Settings → Tax & IRD; Reports → IRD registers).
  6. When the permit (Annex 4) arrives, enter its number in Settings → Tax & IRD, then switch on IRD invoicing and save the café's IRD (CBMS) login.
